# CoLab fonts

Bebas Neue (400) and Space Mono (400, 700) are unmodified, self-hosted Latin
WOFF2 subsets from `@fontsource/bebas-neue@5.3.0` and
`@fontsource/space-mono@5.3.0`, as distributed by
[neocloud-supply-os at 96a6161](https://github.com/yorkerhodes3/neocloud-supply-os/tree/96a6161c6d2b5e70b86c354cf1f164e67d74fec2/docs/assets/fonts).

Both are licensed under the SIL Open Font License 1.1, not MIT or Apache-2.0:

- [Bebas Neue copyright and OFL](Bebas-Neue-OFL.txt): Copyright 2010 Dharma Type.
- [Space Mono copyright and OFL](Space-Mono-OFL.txt): Copyright 2016 The Space Mono Project Authors.

Notices are from the corresponding Google Fonts upstream families at
`5026800714d07231937d92eb4cceabf06e05d74e`. No font binaries were modified.
No external font services are called at runtime. The Latin subsets do not cover
all Unicode glyphs; system fallbacks remain for other characters.
