SUBLEASE

This SUBLEASE (this “Sublease”) is made as of September 22, 2023 (the “Effective Dat e”) by and between Salesforce, Inc., a Delaware corporation (“Sublesso r”), and Anthropic, PBC, a Delaware public benefit corporation doing business in California as Anthropic, Inc. (“Sublessee”).

RECITALS

A.Hart Foundry Square IV, LLC, a Delaware limited liability company (“Prime Lessor”), and Sublessor, are parties to that certain Office Lease Agreement dated December 22, 2016 (the “Original Lease”), as amended by that certain First Amendment to Office Lease Agreement dated December 22, 2017 (the “First Amendment”, collectively with the Original Lease, the “Prime Lease”) whereby Prime Lessor has leased to Sublessor approximately 230,208 rentable square feet of space (the “Subleased Premises”), comprised of the Storage, and a portion of the 1 st floor and all of 2 nd through 10 th floors, of the office building located at 500 Howard Street, San Francisco CA (the “Building”). The Subleased Premises are more particularly described in the Prime Lease. A redacted copy of the Prime Lease is attached hereto as Exhibit A. Capitalized terms used in this Sublease but not defined herein shall have the meanings given them in the Prime Lease.

B.Prime Lessor and Sublessor are parties to that certain Retail Lease Agreement dated January 24, 2018 (as amended, the “Retail Lease”) whereby Prime Lessor has leased to Sublessor approximately 3,425 rentable square feet of retail space (the “Retail Premises”), comprised of Suite 100 of the Ground Floor of the Building. Concurrently with this Sublease, Sublessee and Sublessor executed that certain Sublease of even date herewith whereby Sublessor subleases the Retail Premises to Sublessee on the terms and conditions set forth therein (the “Retail Sublease”).

C.Sublessee desires to sublease from Sublessor the Subleased Premises, and Sublessor desires to sublease the Subleased Premises to Sublessee on the terms, covenants and conditions set forth in this Sublease.
