STOCKHOLDERS’ AGREEMENT

OF

ANTHROPIC, PBC

This Stockholders’ Agreement (this “Agreement”) is effective as of the IPO Closing Date in connection with the Public Offering (as defined below) and is entered into by and among Anthropic, PBC, a Delaware public benefit corporation (the “Company”), Anthropic Founders, LLC, a Delaware limited liability company (the “Founder LLC”), and the Anthropic Long-Term Benefit Trust, a Delaware noncharitable purpose trust (the “Trust”). The Founder LLC and the Trust are collectively referred to herein as the “Stockholders.” Capitalized terms not otherwise defined herein shall have the meaning given to them in the Company’s Amended and Restated Certificate of Incorporation, duly adopted and filed with the Secretary of State of the State of Delaware in connection with the Public Offering as it may be amended or otherwise modified from time to time (the “Restated Certificate”).

RECITALS

WHEREAS, the Trust, as holder of all outstanding shares of the Class T Common Stock, has, and following the completion of the Public Offering will continue to have, certain governance rights with respect to the Company.

WHEREAS, in connection with the Public Offering, the Company has issued or will issue to the Founder LLC one (1) share of Class F Common Stock, which will grant certain governance rights with respect to the Company to the Founder LLC.

WHEREAS, the Restated Certificate provides that, at such times and subject to the conditions described therein, (i) the holder of the Class T Common Stock then outstanding, exclusively and as a separate class, shall be entitled to elect the smallest number of directors that constitutes a majority of the total number of authorized directors of the Company (collectively, the “Class T Directors” and each, a “Class T Director”) and (ii) the Electing Holders, exclusively and voting together as a single class, shall be entitled to elect the balance of the total number of authorized directors of the Company (collectively, the “At-Large Directors” and each, an “At-Large Director”); provided, however, that prior to the Class F Sunset Time, one Class T Director shall be designated by the holder of the Class T Common Stock then outstanding as the “Specified Class T Director” and subject to the other requirements set forth in the Restated Certificate.

WHEREAS, in connection with and to facilitate the completion of the Public Offering, the Company and the Stockholders desire to enter into this Agreement to ensure the governance rights of the parties.

NOW, THEREFORE, the parties agree as follows:

Each Stockholder shall vote, or cause to be voted, all Shares owned by such Stockholder, or over which such Stockholder has voting control (to the extent such Shares are entitled to vote on such matters under the Restated Certificate or applicable law), from time to time and at all times, in whatever manner as shall be necessary and to take all Necessary Action to ensure that at each annual or special meeting of the Company’s stockholders at which an election of directors is held or the removal of any director is acted upon, or pursuant to any written consent of the stockholders, (x) the size and composition of the Board shall be set and remain as described in Sections 2.1 and 2.2 and (y) each nomination and removal of a director for which notice has been duly given, and (if applicable) which has been approved, in accordance with Section 2.5 shall be given effect, including as to the specific individual identified in any such notice of nomination.

2.1 Board Size. Prior to the Class T Sunset Time, the total number of authorized directors of the Company that constitute the Whole Board shall be fixed solely by the Board acting pursuant to a resolution adopted by the affirmative vote of directors representing 80% of the Whole Board, rounded up or down to the nearest whole number (any exact 0.5 rounding up); provided that until the earlier to occur of the Class F Sunset Time or the Class T Sunset Time, the total number of authorized directors of the Company must be an odd number. From and after the Class T Sunset Time, the total number of authorized directors of the Company that constitute the Whole Board shall be fixed solely by resolution of the Board acting pursuant to a resolution adopted by the affirmative vote of directors representing a majority of the Whole Board.
