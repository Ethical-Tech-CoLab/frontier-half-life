ANTHROPIC LONG-TERM BENEFIT TRUST

AMENDED AND RESTATED TRUST AGREEMENT

June 29, 2023 (as amended and restated, [__________]) 

WHEREAS, Anthropic, PBC, a Delaware public benefit corporation (the “Company”), created a trust known as the “Anthropic Long-Term Benefit Trust” (the “Trust”) pursuant to a trust agreement effective June 29, 2023;

WHEREAS, the Company established the Trust to strengthen the Company’s commitment to maintaining a responsible balance among the different elements of the Company’s mission;

WHEREAS, to achieve this purpose, the Company granted the Trust certain rights and powers with respect to the Company; and

WHEREAS, the Trust now desires to enter into this Amended and Restated Trust Agreement (this “Agreement”) to further amend the Amended and Restated Anthropic Long-Term Benefit Trust Agreement (the “Existing Agreement”) to reflect certain changes to the governance and operations of the Trust and the Company.

THEREFORE, the Existing Agreement is hereby amended and restated to provide that the Trust shall be held, administered, and managed in accordance with the following terms: 

1.CREATION; NAME; PURPOSE

1.1.Creation. The Company irrevocably issued and conveyed to the Trustees all of the Company’s authorized shares of Class T Common Stock. The Voting Trustees and Administrative Trustee irrevocably agreed to hold in trust such shares of stock and such other property contributed by the Company pursuant to that certain Resource and Information Agreement entered into by and between the Company and the Trust, acting by and through the Voting Trustees. The Trust shall be a noncharitable purpose trust within the meaning of Section 3556 of Title 12 of the Delaware Code, as amended. In connection with entry into this Agreement, the Trust, the Company, the holder of the Company’s Class F Common Stock and certain other parties are entering into the Stockholders’ Agreement setting forth the terms and conditions pursuant to which the Trust and certain stockholders of the Company will vote their shares of the Company’s capital stock in favor of certain designees to the Board and on certain other matters and to ensure certain other governance rights of the parties thereto.

1.2.Name. The trust administered hereunder shall be known as the “Anthropic Long-Term Benefit Trust”.

1.3.Purpose. The Voting Trustees and Administrative Trustee shall exercise their powers to achieve the Purpose of the Trust. The Purpose of the Trust shall be to ensure, through the exercise of the rights, powers and preferences of the shares of Class T Common Stock
