CLASS N COMMON STOCK PURCHASE AGREEMENT

THIS CLASS N COMMON STOCK PURCHASE AGREEMENT (this “Agreement”) is made as of [●], 2026, by and between Anthropic, PBC, a Delaware public benefit corporation (the “Company”), and [●] (the “Investor”).

THE PARTIES HEREBY AGREE AS FOLLOWS:

1.Purchase and Sale of Stock.

1.1 Sale and Issuance of Class N Common Stock. Subject to the terms and conditions of this Agreement, the Investor agrees to purchase from the Company, and the Company agrees to sell and issue to the Investor, the Shares (as defined below) at a price per share equal to the per share initial public offering price of the Class A Common Stock (as defined below) (before underwriting discounts and expenses) in the IPO (as defined below) (the “IPO Price”). “Shares” means the number of shares of Class N Common Stock of the Company, par value $0.00001 per share (the “Class N Common Stock”), having the rights, powers and restrictions set forth in the form of the Company’s Amended and Restated Certificate of Incorporation filed as Exhibit 3.2 in the Company’s Registration Statement on Form S-1/A filed with the Securities and Exchange Commission on November 2, 2026 (the “Charter”), equal to $[●] divided by the IPO Price, rounded down to the nearest whole share (with the total purchase price correspondingly reduced for such fractional share amount). “IPO” means the Company’s initial public offering of shares of its Class A Common Stock, par value $0.00001 per share (the “Class A Common Stock”), pursuant to an underwriting agreement to be entered into by and among the Company and certain underwriters (the “Underwriters”) (the “Underwriting Agreement”) and the Company’s Registration Statement on Form S-1 (File No. 333-299633) (the “Registration Statement”).

1.2 Closing. The purchase and sale of the Shares shall take place remotely immediately following the closing of the IPO (the “Closing” and the date on which the Closing occurs, the “Closing Date”). At the Closing, the Investor shall make payment of the purchase price of the Shares by wire transfer in immediately available funds to the account specified by the Company in writing at least two (2) business days prior to the Closing and in the amount specified by the Company in writing promptly following the execution of the Underwriting Agreement (and in any event no later than one (1) business day prior to the Closing), against delivery to the Investor of evidence from the Company’s transfer agent of a book entry position evidencing the Shares registered in the name of the Investor. If the Investor has funded all or any portion of the purchase price of the Shares and the Closing does not occur on the Closing Date, the Company shall promptly (and in any event within one (1) business day thereafter) return such funds to the Investor by wire transfer of immediately available funds to the account from which such funds were sent, without any deduction, set-off or counterclaim.

The Company is duly qualified to transact business in each jurisdiction in which it is required to be so qualified, except where the failure to be so qualified would not reasonably be expected to have a material adverse effect on the Company.
